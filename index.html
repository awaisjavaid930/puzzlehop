Welcome to Royal Smash! Puzzle Ball Game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how Royal Smash! Puzzle Ball Game handles your information when you use our application.

Royal Smash! Puzzle Ball Game does not collect personal information from you directly.

We do not collect or process:

  • Your name

Information Third Parties May Collect

While we do not collect personal data, third-party services integrated into our app may collect information for their own purposes:

This may include identifiers for advertising, IP addresses, and general location information. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): We use FCM to send you notifications about game updates, events, and messages. Your device may share an FCM token with our servers for delivery purposes. We do not use FCM to collect personal information.

Your game progress is stored on your device. Login functionality allows you to save your progress, but we do not store personal information beyond what is necessary for account authentication.

How We Use Your Information

Royal Smash! Puzzle Ball Game does not use, process, store, sell, or share personal data that we collect directly from you. We do not share your information with third parties.

However, third-party services (AdMob and FCM) integrated into our application may independently collect and use data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their data practices.

Children's Privacy

Royal Smash! Puzzle Ball Game is suitable for users of all ages. If you are under 13 years old (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), please ensure that a parent or guardian reviews this policy and consents to your use of the application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, but third-party services like AdMob may do so as part of their ad serving practices.

Data Security

Royal Smash! Puzzle Ball Game does not store personal information on servers. Game progress and account information are secured on your device and protected by standard industry practices. However, we encourage you to maintain secure access to your account credentials.
